## 6. UI/UX Design Standards & De-Vibecoding Rules

To maintain high visual craft and avoid artificial "vibecoded" gimmicks, all future mobile screens must adhere to these five design rules:

1. **Commit to a Single Disciplined Aesthetic:**
   * Warm, human, pillowy sanctuary design (inspired by Joyce).
   * Consistent radii (`rounded-3xl` for cards, `rounded-2xl` for secondary panels, `rounded-full` for pills and buttons).
   * Soft, warm canvas (`#FAF7F2`) with muted text (`#756C68`) and rich espresso primary text (`#1A1615`).
2. **Zero Pseudo-Organic CSS Hacks:**
   * Strictly ban asymmetrical compound border-radius formulas (e.g. `border-radius: 46px 36px 52px 30px`).
   * Strictly ban generic floating absolute-positioned emojis (`âœ¨`, `âœ¦`, `â˜…`, `âš¡`) as particle decoration.

   * Use direct, empathetic, real-world language: *"Practice difficult conversations"*, *"How often do you want to practice?"*, *"Pushing back on unrealistic deadlines"*, *"Session feedback"*.
