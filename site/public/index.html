The coaching model

Every debrief is built around C.A.R.E.

Instead of generic tips, HardSync scores and coaches your practice sessions against one consistent framework — so feedback compounds instead of scattering.

  • C

    Connect

    Build an empathetic bridge before you deliver hard news or hold a firm boundary.

  • A

    Acknowledge

    Name what the other person is feeling or has contributed, instead of talking past it.

  • R

    Respond

    Ask questions and address the real issue directly, rather than assuming or accusing.

  • E

    Empower

    Close with a clear next step, decision, or commitment both people can act on.

Good to know

A few common questions.

Is my practice session recorded or stored?+
HardSync does not record or store the audio or video from your live calls — that stream goes only to the call provider needed to run the session. Your transcript and coaching report are saved to your account so you can review them later. See the Privacy Policy for details.
Is this a substitute for real professional advice?+
No. HardSync's AI coaching is practice guidance, not legal, medical, employment, or financial advice, and it shouldn't be used to make high-impact decisions about another person. Full details are in the Terms of Service.
Can I delete my account and data?+
Yes, any time, from your Profile in the app. If you can't sign in, use the web account deletion page instead.
