The basics

What is HardSync?+
HardSync is a rehearsal space for high-stakes workplace conversations — negotiating a deadline, giving hard feedback, holding a boundary with someone senior to you. You practice against a realistic AI counterpart by text, voice, or video, then get a debrief based on what you actually said.
Who is HardSync for?+
New managers moving from peer to manager, engineering and product leaders negotiating priorities, people managers delivering corrective feedback, and senior leaders pushing back on unrealistic requests or negotiating across teams.
What is the C.A.R.E. framework?+
It's the model every debrief is scored against: Connect (build an empathetic bridge first), Acknowledge (name what the other person feels or has contributed), Respond (ask questions and address the real issue directly), and Empower (close with a clear next step both people can act on).
Which platforms is it available on?+
HardSync is built with Flutter for iOS, Android, and web from a single app, so your progress and practice history follow you across devices.

Practice sessions

What's the difference between text, voice, and video practice?+
Text rehearsal is a written back-and-forth with your AI counterpart. Voice rehearsal is a live spoken conversation. Video rehearsal streams a live AI avatar with real-time expressions, so you can also read a face while you talk. Voice requires a Pro or Ultra plan; video is exclusive to Ultra.
Do the AI counterparts actually push back?+
Yes. Each persona has its own baseline defensiveness and pushback style, and responds to whether you escalate or de-escalate — it isn't a scripted bot that agrees with everything you say.
Can I create my own scenario?+
Yes. Custom Scenario Studio lets you brief a real situation you're facing — context, the counterpart's likely defensiveness, your objectives, and any phrases you want to avoid.
What do I get after a session?+
A scorecard covering clarity, empathy, boundary firmness, and presence composure, plus timestamped moments from your own transcript labeled as strong boundary statements or weaker phrasing, and side-by-side suggestions for what to say instead.
Is HardSync coaching a substitute for professional advice?+
No. AI responses may be incomplete or inaccurate, and they're practice guidance, not legal, medical, employment, or financial advice. Don't use HardSync to make high-impact decisions about another person. See the full Terms of Service.

Pricing & credits

What does the Free plan include?+
The full lesson library, progress tracking, and text-based rehearsal, plus a one-time starter balance of practice credits when you create an account.
What do Pro and Ultra add?+
Pro unlocks live audio rehearsal and a monthly credit allowance. Ultra unlocks video calls with AI and a larger monthly allowance. See current plans and pricing on the pricing section — your app store listing always shows the final, up-to-date price.
What are practice credits?+
Credits are an internal usage unit spent per session — text rehearsal costs the least, live audio costs more, and video calls with AI cost the most, roughly in proportion to how much it costs to run. Subscriptions include a monthly credit allowance; you can also buy a top-up credit pack if you run out. Credits have no cash value and aren't transferable.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?+
Subscriptions are billed and managed through your device's app store (Apple or Google), including cancellation and restoring a purchase. Deleting your HardSync account does not cancel store billing — cancel there separately.

Privacy & data

Are my calls recorded?+
No. During a live voice or video session, your microphone or camera stream is sent to the call provider needed to run that session, but HardSync does not record or store call audio or video. Your session transcript and coaching report are saved to your account.
Who else processes my data?+
Providers involved in running the service include Supabase (accounts and data), Google Gemini (AI coaching and dialogue), Tavus and Daily (video avatars), RevenueCat (subscriptions), and Google Mobile Ads. Each processes data under its own terms to provide its part of the service. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
Is HardSync directed at children?+
No. HardSync is a professional communication practice tool and is not directed to children.

Account

How do I delete my account?+
Open Profile in the app and select Delete account. If you can't sign in, use the web account deletion page instead — it walks through deleting from any browser or getting help if you're locked out.
What happens to my data when I delete my account?+
Your progress, sessions, transcripts, reports, credit history, and custom scenarios are deleted. Some transaction, security, fraud-prevention, or legal records may be retained only as long as required by law, then deleted. Store subscriptions must be canceled separately through Apple or Google.
