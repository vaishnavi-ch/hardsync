Delete a HardSync account

This works without reinstalling the mobile app.

Delete from any browser

  1. Open the HardSync web application.
  2. Sign in with the same account.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Select Delete account and confirm.

The request deletes the account and associated progress, sessions, transcripts, reports, credit history, and custom scenarios. HardSync does not store call audio or video.

Store subscriptions are controlled by Apple or Google and must be canceled separately before or after deleting the HardSync account.

If you cannot sign in

Contact the developer using the support contact listed on the Google Play or Apple App Store listing, or email hello@hardsync.app. Include the account email address and ask for account deletion. Do not send your password. Requests will be verified before deletion.

What may be retained

Some transaction, security, fraud-prevention, or legal records may be retained where required by law. They are restricted to those purposes and deleted when the retention requirement ends.
