Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Information we process

We process account details, profile choices, course progress, practice text and transcripts, session results, credit transactions, subscription status, and technical diagnostics. During a live voice or video practice, your microphone or camera stream is sent to the selected call provider so the session can work. By default HardSync does not keep a copy of that stream. Some sessions offer an opt-in "save replay" option; if you turn it on, the recording is uploaded to our cloud storage, kept under your account, and deleted automatically once it expires or you remove it. Session transcripts and reports may be saved to your account regardless of whether you save a replay.

Purposes and providers

We use this information to authenticate users, deliver courses and AI practice, generate requested feedback, maintain progress and credits, prevent abuse, process purchases, and provide support. Providers include Supabase, Google Gemini (text and voice practice), Tavus and Daily (video avatar practice), and RevenueCat (subscriptions and purchases). Providers process data to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Purchases

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process store purchases. HardSync receives transaction and entitlement status but not full payment-card details.

Retention, security, and deletion

We retain account data, transcripts, and reports while needed to provide the service and meet security, fraud-prevention, financial-record, and legal obligations. Live call audio and video are streamed to the selected call provider and are not stored by HardSync unless you opt in to saving a session replay, in which case the recording is retained until it expires or you delete it. Data is encrypted in transit and private records use access controls. You can delete your account in Profile or use the account deletion page.

Your choices

You can deny camera or microphone access, manage subscriptions through your store, and request account deletion. HardSync is a professional communication service and is not directed to children.

Contact

For privacy questions or help with deletion, contact the HardSync developer through the support contact listed on the Google Play or Apple App Store listing, or email vaishnavi26ch@gmail.com.
