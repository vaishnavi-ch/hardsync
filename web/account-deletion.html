Delete a HardSync account

Delete from any browser

  1. Open the HardSync web application.
  2. Sign in with the same account.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Select Delete account and confirm.

This works without reinstalling the mobile app. The request deletes the Supabase account and associated progress, sessions, transcripts, reports, credit history, and custom scenarios. HardSync does not save call audio or video.

Store subscriptions are controlled by Apple or Google and must be canceled separately before or after deleting the HardSync account.

If you cannot sign in

Use the HardSync support channel shown in the application. A verified public support address or form will be added here before store submission.
