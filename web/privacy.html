HardSync Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

Information we process

We process account details, profile choices, course progress, practice text and transcripts, session results, subscription status, and technical diagnostics. During a live voice or video practice, your microphone or camera stream is sent to the selected call provider so the session can work. HardSync does not record, upload, or save call audio or video. Session transcripts and reports may be saved to your account.

Purposes and providers

We use this information to authenticate users, deliver courses and AI practice, generate requested feedback, maintain progress, prevent abuse, process purchases, and provide support. Providers include Supabase, Google Gemini, and RevenueCat. Google Gemini processes live media to deliver audio and video practice under Google's terms and privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process store purchases. HardSync receives transaction and entitlement status but not full payment-card details.

Retention, security, and deletion

We retain account data, transcripts, and reports only while needed to provide the service and meet security, fraud-prevention, financial-record, and legal obligations. Data is encrypted in transit and private records use access controls. HardSync does not save call audio or video. You can delete your account in Profile or use the account deletion page.

Your choices

You can deny camera or microphone access, manage subscriptions through your store, and request account deletion. HardSync is a professional communication service and is not directed to children.

Contact

Use the support channel shown in the HardSync application for privacy questions. A verified support address will be added here before public store submission.
